# Free-written responses — State of the Community Survey 2026

Every free-written response from every respondent who granted full permission to publish their comments, reproduced exactly as written.

---

## What is here, and what is not

**Permission.** The survey asked separately whether a respondent's free-written comments could be publicly cited or published. **Only the 280 respondents who granted full permission appear in this file.** 

**Verbatim.** Responses are reproduced exactly as written: spelling, punctuation, capitalization, emphasis, line breaks and profanity are the respondents'. Nothing is corrected, shortened or tidied, except references to specific website URLs or products.

**Ordering.** Responses are sorted alphabetically within each question. The ordering is therefore different from question to question, and **answers in this file cannot be linked to a single respondent across questions.** No respondent identifier, demographic attribute or row number is published here. 

**Follow-up questions.** Many of these questions were shown only to respondents who gave a particular answer to an earlier question. Where that is the case, the question it follows from is stated, along with the answers that caused it to be shown.

**Excluded questions.**

- **Q3. Please indicate your gender identification:** — Only one respondent answered this question. Publishing it would risk identifying them. 

See [SURVEY-INSTRUMENT.md](https://github.com/thewpcommunitycollective/programs/blob/main/projects/state-of-the-community/2026/SURVEY-INSTRUMENT.md) for the full instrument and [LIMITATIONS.md](https://github.com/thewpcommunitycollective/programs/blob/main/projects/state-of-the-community/2026/LIMITATIONS.md) for known issues.
