# State of the Community Survey Changelog

All changes to the survey instrument are recorded here. Every change is documented to preserve year-over-year comparability.

## [1.0.0] — 2026-08

First wave. Baseline instrument, 231 items, fielded May 27 – July 17, 2026. 431 responses. Details at [`/2026/`](https://github.com/thewpcommunitycollective/projects/tree/144e5b53a122eaffafef33e3a0e0c27e4838d99c/state-of-the-community/2026)

### Known issues for 2027 planning

- **"AI in WordPress" importance item is bimodal.** To be rewritten. Respondents rating it "very important" include people who consider AI an important *problem*. This will break comparability on this item.
- **Nine-column contribution-friction block returned zero responses.** Routing fault to be corrected.
- **Two questions were incorrectly routed.** Routing fault to be corrected.
- **Event-attendance factors to add:** accessibility, health and safety precautions, and destination-country safety. Existing options unchanged, comparability is preserved.
- **Forced-response validation to be removed** or add "not applicable" as answer.
- **Sentiment-change routing gap** (333 answered the yes/no, 356 supplied a direction) to be fixed.
