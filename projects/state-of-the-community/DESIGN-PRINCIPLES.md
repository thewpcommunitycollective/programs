# Design principles

The survey instrument is based on the principles enumerated here. 

## 1. Ask for information

The purpose of this survey is to assess community sentiment in the WordPress open source project by directly asking the individuals who make up that community. 

## 2. Practice anonymity

Anonymity is key to honest responses. In a small community, row-level demographic data could be used to identify individual respondents. We publish only aggregate level data, which provides the information needed to confirm findings and further analyze the data, but prevents the identification of individual respondents.

## 3. Ensure consent is explicit

Only respondents who explicitly granted permission to publish their free-written comments are quoted or published. Free-written responses from respondents who declined full permission are not publicly availability.

## 4. Publish respondents' comments verbatim

When we were given permission to publish respondents free-written comments, quotations are reproduced exactly as written. This includes criticism of the survey itself. 

## 5. Publish the instrument

The full set of questions is published, enabling ongoing comparison with the responses in `SURVEY-INSTRUMENT.md`.

## 6. Publish the changes

In order to facilitate year-over-year comparison, all changes to the instrument are recorded in `CHANGELOG.md`.

## 7. Publish the failures

Survey errors and limitations are openly documented in `LIMITATIONS.md`.

## 8. Report uncertainty

Whenever applicable, significance thresholds, sample size limitations, and other issues are noted and acknowledged.

## 9. Maintain independence

The WP Open Community Collective is not affiliated with the WordPress Foundation or the WordPress open source project.

## 10. Ensure accessibility

The survey is intended to be accessible to all interested respondents, which includes providing translations and upholding accessibility standards.
