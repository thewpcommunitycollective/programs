# The WP Community Collective Standards Program
This repository folder manages The WP Community Collective's Standards. Standards are frameworks, guidelines, and benchmarks that support The WPCC's mission and values. These efforts are designed to complement our direct funding programs by establishing clear, community-driven norms that strengthen open source ecosystems and support open source contributors.

- Version management
- Public reports and updates

## Contributing

The WPCC welcomes and encourages collaboration from individuals and organizations aligned with our mission and values. Contribution to the public repositories of The WP Community Collective are open to the public. All engagement with and contributions to these repositories are subject to the policies outlined in [The WPCC Code of Conduct](https://www.thewpcommunitycollective.com/about/code-of-conduct/).

To get involved or learn more:
- [Visit our website](https://www.thewpcommunitycollective.com/)
- [Join The WPCC](https://www.thewpcommunitycollective.com/join/)

For questions or inquiries, contact [info@thewpcommunitycollective.com](mailto:info@thewpcommunitycollective.com).
